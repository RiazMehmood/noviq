# PROJECT OVERVIEW

The system is an OFFLINE Legal AI Assistant.

Primary purpose:

Answer questions from Sindh Government documents with extremely high factual accuracy and precise citations.

The assistant MUST NEVER fabricate legal information.

If evidence is missing, it must explicitly state that the answer cannot be determined from the available documents.

Accuracy is significantly more important than creativity.
